What planned transmission upgrades do to PJM congestion, node by node

PJM's FTR credit feed publishes congestion for every node and month under two network topologies: a baseline, and a PROMOD case adjusted for planned transmission upgrades. The map is the difference — upgrade-adjusted minus baseline, $/MWh. Red means PJM's upgraded grid is more congested there.

What this is not

Both series are modelled inputs to PJM's FTR credit calculator. etl/validate.py reconciles them against raw hourly day-ahead LMPs and shows the baseline is not realized outturn — so this is topology versus topology, not model versus reality. Zone shapes are utility service territories, not PJM's network model: PJM classifies node and line locations as CEII and publishes no coordinates.
