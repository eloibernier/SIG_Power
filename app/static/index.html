What planned transmission upgrades do to PJM congestion, node by node

For every pricing node and month, PJM's FTR credit feed publishes congestion under two network topologies: a baseline, and a PROMOD case adjusted for planned transmission upgrades. This maps the difference — upgrade-adjusted minus baseline, in $/MWh of congestion. Positive means PJM's upgraded grid is more congested at that node, not less.
Both series are modelled. Neither is realized outturn — see etl/validate.py, which reconciles them against raw hourly day-ahead LMPs and shows they do not match.
